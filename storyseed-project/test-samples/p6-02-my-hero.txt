Sample code: P6-02
Level: P6
Title: My Hero

My mother is my hero. She is thirty-eight years old. She has an oval face and her eyes are brown. She can make delicious meals for me.

My mother is a housewife. She has done her job for eleven years. She doesn’t have time for leisure because she needs to take care of my brother, sister and me. She needs to take care of my dad too! She needs to cook meals for us, take care of us and do a lot of housework every day. She enjoys her job because we are all very healthy and happy.

Housewives needs to be attentive and hard-working. They need to look after the family. They always help their children to solve the problems so they need to be attentive. Housewives need to do a lot of housework every day so they need to work hard. My mother is a perfect housewife.

My mother always shares her working experience with me. One afternoon, my sister didn’t go home after school when she got off the school bus. Mum was worried so she went to find her in the area around. Finally, my mother found her at her classmate’s home. They were playing. Mum was angry. I was angry about my naughty sister too.

I think my mother is doing an important job. Without her, nobody looks after me because my dad is not attentive or patient. He can’t cook either! Thanks Mum a lot.
