# Proofreading Studio 三篇 P6 測試報告

> 本報告使用三篇已匿名化的 P6 學生作文，採用同一套形成性評估 rubric。分數只用於教學回饋，不應視為正式校內成績。

## 一、逐篇結果

| 樣本 | 題目 | 總分 | Ideas & Voice | Structure | Language | Revision |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| P6-01 | An Unlucky Day | 70/100 | 14/20 | 15/20 | 13/20 | 14/20 |
| P6-02 | My Hero | 75/100 | 16/20 | 15/20 | 14/20 | 15/20 |
| P6-03 | My Hero | 78/100 | 17/20 | 17/20 | 14/20 | 14/20 |

### P6-01｜An Unlucky Day

**優點**
- Clear sequence of events (Last Sunday, Then, Finally) that makes the story easy to follow.
- Direct speech is used well and makes Anna’s reactions lively.
- Length and vocabulary are appropriate for Primary 6.

**下一步**
- Vary sentence beginnings and add one or two small details (place, time, or Anna’s feelings) to make the story more vivid.
- Avoid repeating similar words (for example, 'mad and angry'); show emotion with one strong word or a short action.
- Check a few grammar points: verb forms (e.g., 'it broke' vs 'it was broken') and article use ('a homework assignment').

**Proofreading 分類**

| 分類 | 原文片段 | 教學建議 |
|---|---|---|
| grammar | walking while she was playing on her mobile phone | Make the verb structure simpler: try 'walking while playing on her mobile phone' or 'walking and playing on her mobile phone.' |
| grammar | it was broken | Use the clearer verb form 'it broke' (active) or change to 'her phone was broken' if you want passive. 'It broke' is more direct here. |
| word_choice | She got mad and angry | Avoid repetition: pick one word (e.g. 'angry') or show the feeling with an action: 'She felt angry and stormed home.' |
| grammar | one of the homework | Use 'a homework assignment' or 'one of her homework assignments' to make the noun phrase correct. |
| clarity | Her mum punished her and used Anna’s money to buy her a new mobile phone | Clarify how the punishment worked: for example, 'Her mum punished her by making Anna pay for the new phone with her own money.' |
| punctuation | tried to turn on the television but it was out of order | Add a comma before 'but': 'tried to turn on the television, but it was out of order.' This joins the two clauses correctly. |

### P6-02｜My Hero

**優點**
- Clear main idea: your mother is presented as the writer’s hero.
- Good use of a personal example (the story about finding your sister) to show care and concern.
- Simple, direct sentences that make the essay easy to understand.

**下一步**
- Fix small grammar mistakes (like subject–verb agreement) and improve word order in one sentence.
- Vary sentence beginnings and verbs so the writing does not repeat the same phrase many times.
- Combine short sentences to make paragraphs flow better and reduce repetition (for example, join related duties into one sentence).

**Proofreading 分類**

| 分類 | 原文片段 | 教學建議 |
|---|---|---|
| grammar | Housewives needs to be attentive | Change 'needs' to 'need' to match the plural subject: 'Housewives need...'. |
| grammar | didn’t go home after school when she got off the school bus | Change the order to 'didn't go home after she got off the school bus' or 'didn't go home after getting off the school bus' for clearer meaning. |
| clarity | in the area around | Use a clearer phrase such as 'in the nearby area' or 'around the neighbourhood'. |
| word_choice | solve the problems | Remove 'the' and write 'solve problems' or be specific: 'solve their problems'. |
| structure | She needs to | Avoid repeating 'She needs to' at the start of many sentences; join some ideas (e.g. 'She also looks after my dad, cooks meals, and does housework') and use different verbs. |
| punctuation | Thanks Mum a lot. | Use commas for direct address or write 'Thank you, Mum.' or 'Thanks, Mum.' |
| clarity | Mum was angry. I was angry about my naughty sister too. | Consider combining these short sentences to show the connection, for example: 'Mum was angry, and I was angry about my naughty sister too.' |

### P6-03｜My Hero

**優點**
- Clear main idea: your aunt as a hero and teacher is easy to understand.
- Good use of a short story (the student who pushed her) to support your point.
- Paragraphs are organised: introduction, job details, qualities, an example, and a conclusion show a logical order and development of ideas.

**下一步**
- Vary repeating phrases (for example, avoid using "loves her job very much" several times).
- Check verbs and prepositions (e.g. 'fell' vs 'felt', and 'back to school').
- Use more precise verbs or expressions (for example, 'wears glasses' instead of 'is a woman with glasses'). Read your writing aloud to spot awkward sentences.

**Proofreading 分類**

| 分類 | 原文片段 | 教學建議 |
|---|---|---|
| word_choice | super hero | Write 'superhero' as one word in this context. |
| word_choice | make her become a knowledgeable person | Change to a shorter phrase such as 'help her become knowledgeable' or 'make her knowledgeable' to sound more natural. |
| word_choice | is a woman with glasses | Use a clearer verb: 'She wears glasses.' |
| word_choice | teach them knowledge | Use 'teach them' or 'teach them new things' instead of 'teach them knowledge.' |
| clarity | she still loves her job very much | Avoid repeating the same phrase. Use different expressions (e.g. 'she still enjoys teaching' or 'she continues to love her work'). |
| grammar | she will talk to them politely | Keep verb tenses consistent. In this present-time description use 'she talks to them politely' or 'she always speaks to them politely.' |
| grammar | felt down on the floor | Use 'fell down' or 'fell to the floor' (not 'felt down'). |
| grammar | went back school | Include the preposition: 'went back to school.' |

## 二、三篇共同觀察

三篇總共標記 21 個可教學處理的問題。最常見分類如下：

| 分類 | 次數 | 解讀 |
|---|---:|---|
| grammar | 8 | 主語與動詞一致、時態及介詞等基礎準確度。 |
| word_choice | 6 | 自然搭配、重複用語及更精準的動詞／片語。 |
| clarity | 4 | 句子意思或表達關係需要更清楚。 |
| punctuation | 2 | 直接稱呼、標點及句子連接。 |
| structure | 1 | 句子重複、段落流暢度及事件組織。 |

## 三、對網站的調整建議

第一，Proofreading Studio 應把 AI 回饋分成「必須先修正」與「可選擇改善」兩層，避免小學生看到太多紅色問題而失去信心。
第二，Rubric 分數應固定顯示為形成性評估，並同時展示兩項作品優點及兩至三個下一步；不要只顯示一個總分。
第三，系統應允許導師關閉不適合當堂課的分類。例如 P5 課堂可先集中 grammar、word choice 及 punctuation，P6 再加入 structure 與 clarity。
第四，AI 不應自動把建議改寫回學生作品。學生必須自行修改，再保存為新版本，這樣才能保留原創聲音及修訂紀錄。
第五，三篇樣本都是 P6，尚不足以驗證 P5／P6 的跨年級公平性；下一輪應加入至少一篇 P5，並用相同 rubric 比較評分差異。
