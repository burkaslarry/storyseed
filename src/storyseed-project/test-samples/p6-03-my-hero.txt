Sample code: P6-03
Level: P6
Title: My Hero

My aunt is really my super hero. She is twenty-eight years old. She is a woman with glasses. She has long hair and big eyes too. She is good-looking. She likes reading books very much because she believes reading can make her become a knowledgeable person.

My aunt is a primary school teacher. She has been a teacher for two years. She needs to look after children. Also, she needs to teach them knowledge. She prepares lots of teaching materials before she has lessons. She enjoys her job very much because she loves children and they always make her smile. Although being a teacher is not an easy job, she still loves her job very much.

Teachers need to be kind, patient and caring. Sometimes, some students are very rude and say a lot of bad words. Although it makes her angry, she never shouts at them. She will talk to them politely. She also needs to be caring. When someone is sad, she needs to make them happy and laugh. My aunt works very hard and she is patient enough to be a good teacher.

My aunt always shares her working experience with me. Once, she took care of a student because he got hurt. However, that student cried loudly and pushed her. She felt down on the floor. She was upset. Luckily, another teacher saw that and came to help. She was not seriously hurt. When she went back school the next day, that student said sorry to her. Finally, she forgave him because she thought he did not really want to hurt her.

I think my aunt and all the teachers are doing a meaningful and wonderful job. Without them, we cannot learn different kinds of knowledge. Thanks my aunt and all the teachers.
