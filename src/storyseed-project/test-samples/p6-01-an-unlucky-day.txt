Sample code: P6-01
Level: P6
Title: An Unlucky Day

Last Sunday, Anna was walking while she was playing on her mobile phone. Suddenly, she accidentally dropped her mobile phone on the ground and it was broken! She shouted, ‘Oh no! My parents will be mad! I only bought this phone yesterday!’

Then she went back home and tried to turn on the television but it was out of order. She got mad and angry. She remembered that she needed to borrow a book to finish one of the homework so she went to the library. When she wanted to borrow the book, she couldn’t find her library card! She shouted, ‘I left my library card at home!’

Finally, she went back home but her mum was already there. Her mum punished her and used Anna’s money to buy her a new mobile phone. Her mum said, ‘You should be more careful next time!’
